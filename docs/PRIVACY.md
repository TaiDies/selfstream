# Selfstream privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

Selfstream is a music player app. It connects to a music server that **you** choose and run (or that someone you
trust runs for you). The developer of the app does not operate that server and does not receive any data from the app.

## What the app does with your data

| Data | Where it goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Server address, user name and password | Stored on your device. The password is never sent as plain text: each request carries a one-time salted hash (the Subsonic token login). They are sent only to the server you entered. | To sign in to your server |
| Your music library, playlists, liked songs, search terms | Requested from your server and shown in the app. Searches are sent to your server. | To browse and play your music |
| Listening history (what you played, for how long) | Sent only to your server, and only if the server offers it and the setting "Record my listening" is on. | For your own statistics and recommendations on your server |
| Saved pages, covers and songs you choose to keep offline | Stored on your device only | So the app works without a connection |
| App settings (theme, lyrics style and similar) | Stored on your device only | To remember your preferences |

## What the app does not do

- It has no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
- It does not send anything to the developer or to any third party.
- It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, camera or files outside its own storage.

## Your server

What your server stores or shares with other services (for example search or lyric providers) is decided by whoever
runs it. Ask them, or read their policy. If you run the server yourself, you control that data.

## Purchases (Google Play version)

If you choose to support the app with an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the payment. The app only asks Google
Play whether your account has made the purchase. It does not see your payment details.

## Deleting your data

Sign out in the app's settings to remove the saved server address and login from the device. Uninstalling the app
removes everything it stored. Data on your server stays there until you delete it from the server.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
